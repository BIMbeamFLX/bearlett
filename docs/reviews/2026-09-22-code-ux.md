# Bearlett code review: what hurts the player

22 September 2026. Read of `G:\Github\bearlett` only.

Status: `feat/session-bar` now keeps one strip on the wallet, Notes, the
collection, and the web wallet. Leaving the sats napplet writes the open
payment, note, or handover into the encrypted vault and puts it back after
unlock. A handover names the copy. A pasted card shows its name before
Redeem. A pack bought on tcg.nappelin.com still has to be pasted; that site
is outside this repo. The rest of this note is the review that change was
written against.

Scope is the three surfaces a person can actually open: the web wallet
(`index.html`, `src/pages/`, `src/components/`), the sats napplet
(`src/napplet/main.tsx` and its tools), and the collection napplet
(`src/napplet/collection/`). The Hangar file is a prototype and is treated as
one in the marketing note.

The money core is the part to protect. Journals, mint-string matching, artwork
hash checks, the supply-chain verifier, and the rule that a timeout never pays
twice are doing the job a bearer wallet has to do. The failures below are in
the session a person has, not in that core. Fix the session without loosening
the core.

## The session, in the order a person hits it

1. They install or open the repo and land in a product named LNURLwallet.
2. The sats napplet locks and clears the task the moment they leave it to pay
   or to copy a token.
3. A card bought on the site still has to be exported by hand and pasted.
4. The binder can show a stack and then hand over a single unnamed copy, with
   no review of which card is leaving.
5. The emotional peak of the game (pack, reveal, set progress, a card big
   enough to admire) is not in the shipping collection.

## P0 — the session breaks

### 1. Leaving the sats napplet destroys the task

`src/napplet/main.tsx` locks on `visibilitychange` when the document is hidden
(lines 126–128 and 308) and again on `pagehide` (line 309). `lock()` (lines
97–124) drops the password and also the in-progress receive text, invoice,
payment address, amount, funding invoice, and backup text.

On a phone, paying a Lightning invoice means switching apps. Copying a
`cashuB` token means switching apps. Both fire `visibilitychange`. The person
comes back to a locked wallet and an empty form. A funding invoice can still
exist on the note (`Show stored invoice`, around line 937), but nothing puts
them back on that invoice. The 5-minute auto-lock in
`src/napplet/preferences.ts` never gets a chance to matter.

What to change: keep the staged receive, the staged payment, and the funding
invoice inside the encrypted vault, and restore that exact review after
unlock. Keep the lock. Stop treating an app switch as "throw away the form."

Done when: start a mint quote, switch to another app long enough to pay it,
come back, unlock, and the same invoice is on screen without hunting for it.
Same for a half-pasted receive.

### 2. A purchased card does not arrive in the collection

`src/napplet/collection/receive.ts` lines 35–36, shown under the address in
`src/napplet/collection/main.tsx` around line 1139:

> A card bought on tcg.nappelin.com is locked to that site's wallet: send it
> to your collection's address in the wallet there first, then paste the token
> into the collection.

That is three products and two pastes for one pack. Redeem (`main.tsx`
`redeem`, lines 578–600) then answers with a sentence (`Received N cards.`)
and no art. The grid is the first time the card is visible.

What to change: one action on the site, "Open in my collection," that delivers
`napplet:collection/receive` (the queue already exists: `deliver` /
`stageNext` in `main.tsx`). The collection then shows the card face before
Redeem, and Redeem is the only confirmation. Until that exists, the website
wallet and this napplet are not one game.

Done when: a test purchase ends on the card face inside this napplet, and the
paste paragraph is gone from the first-run sheet.

### 3. Hand-over can give away the wrong copy, and it does not say which

Selection is by catalogue id, one toggle per stack (`toggle` / `open`,
`main.tsx` lines 623–634). The button count is the number of stacks (line
908). `hand()` (lines 639–651) always spends `stack.items[0]` and no other
copy. A stack badged `×5` still sends one proof. The sheet says "Handing over
N cards" and never names the card. There is no second step that shows the
face, the copy, and the address together. Close does not ask. The loop has no
per-card result: if the second id throws, the first has already left, and the
only new text is the error from `readable()`.

What to change: the review sheet lists every copy by name and face, states
how many remain, and requires a second press. A stack of five needs an
explicit "this copy" or "all five." A partial failure lists which copies
left and which stayed.

Done when: a stack of two cannot lose a copy without the sheet having shown
that card's name and face, and a failed second card reports the first as
already handed over.

### 4. The installed app is still the upstream wallet

| Surface | What a person reads | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browser tab and iOS home-screen name | LNURLwallet | `index.html` lines 20–21 |
| Installed PWA name | LNURLwallet | `vite.config.ts` lines 78–80 |
| PWA description | Bearlett — Bearer Asset Wallet… | `package.json` `description`, fed into that same manifest |
| Nav brand | LNURLwallet | `src/components/Nav.tsx` line 41 |
| Landing title | Your LNURLcash wallet | `src/pages/Hero.tsx` lines 24–29 |
| In-app docs | What LNURLwallet is | `src/pages/Docs.tsx` lines 10–16 |
| Footer | LNURLwallet, link to lnurlcash.com, link to `lnurlcash/lnurl-wallet` | `src/components/Footer.tsx` lines 47–64 |

`npm run dev` and `npm run build` serve this app. The Bearlett name lives on
the napplets and in the README. The footer sends a person who needs help to
another project's site and another project's GitHub.

The landing page also sells an "Optional hardware vault" (`Hero.tsx` lines
89–96) while the README's V1 scope says Cashu hardware custody is out. Camera
and NFC exist here and are explicitly absent from the sats napplet
(`src/napplet/WalletTools.tsx` line 601: "Use the webwallet for camera
scanning and NFC.").

What to change: decide which binary is the product. If the napplets are the
product, the repo root should open those, under the name Bearlett, with a
footer that points at `BIMbeamFLX/bearlett`. If the web wallet stays as the
LNURLcash compatibility app, label it that way on its own screen and stop
using it as the front door. One install name, one help link.

Done when: the installed name, the tab title, the first heading, and the
footer repository are the same product.

## P1 — the collection is a binder, and a thin one

### 5. Home shows what you hold. It hides the set you are chasing.

`catalog` is loaded (`main.tsx` line 291) and used for issued counts and
`scarcityRatio` on the back of a card. Nothing renders the cards you do not
hold. Counters are Cards, Distinct, Duplicates, and Packs issued. There is
no "12 of N in the set," no missing list, and no pack.

The empty state (`main.tsx` lines 922–929) says "Clear the filters" when a
search misses. There is no Clear control. The person has to reset search,
tier, type, and Duplicates by hand.

What to change: a set view with owned and missing, a single Clear on the
empty state, and the Receive address visible before any card exists. The
address is currently behind the Receive sheet (lines 1120–1138).

### 6. The card you open is smaller than the card in the grid, and the back is a lab report

`.flipper` is `min(280px, 60vw)` (`collection.css` lines 448–451). On a
390px-wide phone the grid column is about the content width (~350px) and the
opened card is 60vw (~234px). The detail view shrinks the thing you came to
see.

The back (`main.tsx` lines 1018–1052) shows Asset id, a truncated binding, a
truncated face hash, and `stack.items[0].state` raw (`UNSPENT` and friends).
One green word, "checked," is the whole provenance story a player needs. The
rest belongs under a disclosure.

Foil does not follow the edition you ship. `FOIL_BELOW = 60` (`main.tsx`
lines 62 and 109). Edition One Genesis is documented at 63 copies
(`docs/HOW-TO-MINT-ASSETS.md` lines 61–63), so those chase cards never get
`data-foil="true"`. Edition G's Genesis titles are documented at most 3
copies, so they do. The shimmer itself only runs on `:hover`
(`collection.css` lines 276–291). A thumb never sees it. The unit fixture
uses a Genesis of 3 copies (`cards.test.ts` lines 28–33), so the suite never
notices the Edition One miss.

What to change: the opened card fills the sheet. Provenance is one sentence
plus an optional proof. Foil is a tier from the catalogue (Genesis, and
whatever else the census calls scarce), and it animates once on appear, with
`prefers-reduced-motion` already respected at `collection.css` line 565.

### 7. Trade across a table is a text area

The sats napplet draws a QR for a handed-over note (`main.tsx` lines
960–969). The collection copies a long address and then a `cashuB` blob
(`main.tsx` lines 1123–1148 and 1241–1262). Placeholder text is `cashuB…`.
There is no camera, no QR, and no "they have it" signal other than the sender
pressing "They were passed on." Clipboard already fails closed in a sandbox
(`copyAddress`, lines 603–620); the fallback is select-and-hope.

What to change: the collection address and each outgoing token get a QR, and
the sender's sheet stays open until the recipient's collection has redeemed
or the sender deliberately dismisses it. Keep the rule that the token remains
stored until they say it was passed on.

### 8. Dialogs are announced and then ignored

The detail, receive, and hand-over sheets set `role="dialog"` and
`aria-modal="true"` (`main.tsx` lines 993–998, 1079–1085, 1192–1198).
`open()` does not move focus into the sheet. Nothing listens for Escape.
Nothing restores focus to the card that was opened. A keyboard user tabbles
through the page behind the overlay.

What to change: focus the Close control on open, trap Tab inside the sheet,
Escape closes (and still asks before discarding a token, which `closeReceive`
already does), and focus returns to the card.

### 9. The collection has no small-screen layout and no safe area

`collection.css` has one media query, `prefers-reduced-motion` (line 565).
The toolbar is a wrapping row of search, two selects, and four buttons
(`.console`, lines 146–151). Sheets use a fixed inset and 20px of padding
(lines 412–419). Nothing in `src/` reads `env(safe-area-inset-*)` or
`100dvh`. On a phone with a home indicator, Close and Hand over sit in the
obscured band. Wallet napplet buttons do shrink under 640px
(`src/napplet/style.css` lines 772–801) and still have no safe area.

Card hover lifts 10px on `:hover` and `:focus-visible` together
(`collection.css` lines 247–251), with no `@media (hover: hover)`. On touch,
the first contact can leave the card translated.

What to change: a real phone layout (toolbar becomes Receive and Hand over,
filters behind one control), safe-area padding on the shell and the sheets,
and the lift only on hover-capable pointers.

## P1 — the sats napplet teaches the wrong model of money

### 10. The big number is not a balance you can spend

`src/napplet/main.tsx` lines 544–555 add every `ready` note into one sat
figure. The line under it says balances stay separate by mint. They do:
combine and pay are per mint. `groupByMint` defaults to true
(`preferences.ts` line 15) and only affects sort order (`visibleNotes`,
lines 330–346). It does not change the headline.

What to change: the headline is per mint, largest spendable mint first, with
the mint's name. A combined figure, if you keep one, is labelled as a sum of
separate notes.

### 11. First-run copy points at the wrong action

The empty wallet (`main.tsx` lines 740–750) says "Receive a note from
someone, or mint one by paying a Lightning invoice" and offers one button,
Receive. "Paying an invoice" is how people send money. Minting is how this
wallet receives it. The Receive tab then asks them to paste
`lnurlw://… · LNURL1… · cashuA… · cashuB…` (lines 984–991).

Spent notes, once history is on, are labelled "not outstanding" (lines
902–904). Every other status is the raw enum.

What to change: two buttons, Receive and Add sats, and the word "mint" only
after the mint's name is on screen. Status words: ready, pending, spent,
shared.

### 12. Protocol controls sit on the primary screen

Selecting a note reveals Rotate, Combine, Hand over, and a format menu of
HTTPS note, LNURL, LNURLw, and Webwallet claim link (`main.tsx` lines
807–823). "Import design" opens a box labelled Design JSON (lines 693–727)
even though Notes exists to push appearance through `wallet/design`. Activity,
recovery, and settings live in "More wallet tools" (lines 575–605), a
`<select>` that does not light up any of the five tabs while you are inside
it.

What to change: Hand over produces one QR and one link. The other formats
move under a disclosure. Design import leaves this screen; Notes is the only
way in. Activity is a tab. The select stops being the map of the product.

## P2 — trust and polish

- Artwork failure renders the words "no face" or "loading"
  (`main.tsx` lines 111–115). Use "Artwork unavailable" and keep the card name
  on the face.
- The supply sentence on the home screen ("Supply attested …, snapshot N,
  signed by the issuer and checked here," lines 717–722) is verifier output.
  The player line is the counter "Packs issued." Move the attestation next
  to the proof on the card back. When the chain fails, keep the current
  behaviour: say why, show no counts, leave the cards up.
- `readable()` (`main.tsx` lines 83–91) already rewrites two host errors into
  holder language. Many other failures still pass `error.message` through.
  Do that same rewrite for every string that contains nutft, NUT, proof,
  keyset, or counter.
- Collection display type is a system stack that names Oswald, Arial Narrow,
  and Helvetica Neue (`collection.css` lines 8–13 and 27–30), because the
  file comments say a display face is not bundled yet. Wallet and Notes
  already inline Josefin Sans and IBM Plex Mono (`style.css` lines 18–105)
  and the collection does not import that file. Ship one display face in the
  collection artifact. The comment at the top of `collection.css` is stale
  relative to the wallet.
- The Hangar prototype loads Google Fonts and lists Roboto in `--sys`
  (`docs/prototype/bearlett-hangar.html` lines 3 and 17). The design brief
  forbids Roboto, and a napplet cannot fetch fonts. Do not promote that file
  to a shell without replacing both.

## Leave these alone

- Do not unlock across app switches by keeping secrets in page memory. Resume
  from the vault.
- Do not auto-redeem a delivered token. The holder presses Redeem. Show the
  face first.
- Do not drop a handed-over token when the sheet closes. `passedOn` is the
  right gate (`main.tsx` lines 662–669).
- Do not normalise `BEARLETT_MINT`. `editions.ts` `checkMintAddress` refuses
  a trailing slash because the wallet compares mint strings character for
  character.
- Do not delete unreadable or invalid tokens. The "Not shown" notice (lines
  965–984) is the right behaviour. Give it plainer words, and keep the
  reasons.
- Account wallets stay behind `BEARLETT_ACCOUNT_WALLETS=1`
  (`docs/NAPPLETS.md`, `editions.ts`). The alpha build must keep refusing to
  offer a move. The move copy ("Move your cards to your account") only
  belongs on that flag, and it needs a glossary line: this account is the
  shell identity, and the cards are still bearer proofs.

## What I did not verify

The README's "509 passing, 1 skipped, 10 Sep 2026" was not re-run. Treat it
as a dated log line. The collection preview host does exist; that claim is
covered in the marketing note because it is a public-doc defect, and the
code for it is `scripts/napplet-host.mjs` plus `npm run preview:collection`.
